Dinosaur Eggy Countdown

Join the fun as ten little dinosaur eggys wait for a big surprise. One by one, the eggs begin to crack and hatch, revealing adorable baby dinosaurs ready to explore the world.

Told in playful rhymes, this colorful counting adventure makes storytime exciting for little ones who love dinosaurs, discovery, and learning through rhythm.

Rocketship Blast Off

Count down with real NASA rockets, from Mercury to the Space Shuttle and beyond to Artemis, in this fun and engaging picture book for young space lovers.

With playful rhymes, fun facts, and bold illustrations, this read-aloud adventure brings space missions to life for toddlers and preschoolers who love rockets and blast offs.

About the authors

A husband-and-wife team inspired to create the books they wanted for their own family.

John and Paige Nemec are the husband-and-wife duo behind Eggy Books. As they searched for fun, engaging books for their young children, they could not always find the exact stories they were looking for, so they decided to create their own.

Together, they write playful picture books that are fun to read aloud and made to support early learning. John also illustrates the books, helping bring each story to life with warmth, color, and imagination.
